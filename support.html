Support

Questions, problems and how to get your records out.

Write to engr.kamalnasir@gmail.com. Say which iPhone and which iOS version, and what you expected to happen.

A day is in the wrong place

Open the year sheet, tap the day, and change the region. A day you set by hand is never overwritten by automatic counting. If you were in two countries that day, turn on Split this day and name both.

Automatic counting is not finding my days

The visits service reports a stay after you have settled somewhere, not the moment you arrive, so a day can appear late. Check that Location Services is on for Tenure and set to Always, and that Low Power Mode is off. Days you record yourself always count immediately.

I crossed a border at midnight

Tenure asks when your timezone changes, because that is usually a border. Answer it on the day sheet and the day is dated where it happened, not where your phone is now.

Getting your records out

Records, then Export. A JSON backup holds everything and can be imported back into the app. A CSV has one row per day. The PDF report is the one to hand to an accountant: it carries a cover, a summary per rule, a stays page, the day list and a legend saying which days you confirmed yourself.

Restoring a purchase

Records, then Restore Purchases. If nothing is found, check you are signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

A rule looks wrong

Every rule names its source and the date it was last read. Open the source and compare. If the page has changed, write in and it will be re-read and dated again in the next update.
