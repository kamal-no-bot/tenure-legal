Tenure

A tax residency day counter that keeps your days on your phone.

Tenure counts the days that decide tax residency under rules you choose: the US substantial presence test, the UK statutory residence test, the Schengen short stay rule, a 183 day country rule, or a US state statutory residency rule. Days are recorded by hand or counted by the low power visits service, resolved to a country or a state on the device, and confirmed by you before they count.

What it is not

Tenure counts days and shows those counts against published thresholds. It does not determine your tax residence and it does not give tax or legal advice. Every rule it ships names the page it was read from and the date that page was last read, so you can check it yourself and take the count to somebody qualified.

Nothing leaves the phone

Tenure makes no network requests at all. There is no account, no sign in and no server, so your location history has nowhere to go.
