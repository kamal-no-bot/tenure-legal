What Tenure does, and what it does not do

Tenure counts days of presence under rules you select and shows those counts against published thresholds. It does not determine your tax residence, and nothing in it is tax or legal advice. A count is a record of days. What that count means for you depends on facts the app does not have and on law that changes, and it is a question for a qualified adviser.

Rules and their sources

Every rule shipped with the app names the page it was read from and the date it was last read. Those pages are published by tax authorities and can change without notice. Check the source before relying on a threshold. Rules you define yourself carry no source, because you entered them.

Accuracy

Automatic counting uses the operating system's own visits service, which is designed for low power rather than for completeness. It can miss a stay, place one late, or place one near a border on the wrong side. That is why the app asks you to confirm what it detected and never treats a detected day as a confirmed one. The record is yours and the responsibility for its accuracy is yours.
