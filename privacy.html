Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Tenure collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no third party SDK and no server. The app makes no network requests at all, so nothing it holds can be transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your device

  • Your days. One record per day: a country or state code, the date, how the day was recorded and whether you confirmed it.
  • Your rules. Which rules you track, which one is pinned, and your home region.
  • Visit events, while they are being resolved. When automatic counting is on, the system's low power visits service reports that you settled somewhere. Tenure works out which country or state that is, on the device, against a boundary file that ships inside the app, and keeps the resulting DAY. It does not keep a trail of coordinates.

What is never collected

No name, no email address, no contacts, no advertising identifier, no device identifier, and no record of anywhere you have been. There is no crash reporting SDK and no analytics SDK in the app. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected because that is literally true.

Location

Location is optional and the whole app works with it refused: you record days by hand in two taps and every rule, the year sheet, the planner and the report keep working. If you turn automatic counting on, Tenure uses the CoreLocation visits and significant change services only. It never uses continuous GPS and it declares no background location mode. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

What you export

Exports and the PDF report carry country and state codes and dates. They never carry coordinates. Notes are included only if you tick the box that says so. Where a file goes after you export it is up to you.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Tenure never sees your payment details. The app stores only whether a purchase is active, on your device.

Children

Tenure is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it holds goes with it. There is no copy anywhere else, because there was never a copy anywhere else. You can also export a backup first.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the previous version stays in this page's history on GitHub.
